Frequently asked

Essential information.

How does booking work?

All bookings are made through direct contact only. Message us privately to check availability and receive full details.

What is the minimum stay?

The minimum stay varies by season. Contact us to check your dates.

Are services included?

Regular housekeeping and support are included during your stay. Private chef, 24h concierge and transfer are available on request.

Are pets allowed?

Each request is assessed individually. Contact us to discuss the conditions.

How is check-in?

A personal check-in with a full tour of the house and instructions. Times agreed at booking.

Is the house available for events?

Subject to review and approval. Private celebrations and corporate gatherings are assessed case by case.
